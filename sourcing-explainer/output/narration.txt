Scene 1, Every week, a full board (12.2 s)
Every week, 150 vendors pin fresh signals to our sourcing board. Contracts, renewals, security reviews, usage, market news. It piles up fast, and the important ones get buried.

Scene 2, From noise to priorities (11.8 s)
The Supplier Intelligence Hub reads every pin and sorts the board. Most can wait. 18 need action now, and one of them is our fictional Vendor X.

Scene 3, From a clause to dollars (13.8 s)
Its renewal draft quietly lifts the price cap from 2 to 6 percent. On 1 million dollars of spend, the ceiling jumps from 20,000 to 60,000. That is 40,000 dollars a year.

Scene 4, From a deadline to a plan (12.2 s)
The hub tags it Increase Leverage, with 90 days left before renewal. The team asks to restore the 2 percent cap and uses the time to compare options.

Scene 5, From reacting to seeing ahead (11.8 s)
Caught early, a surprise cost becomes a negotiation. Every week, the board gets sorted before it piles up, so nothing costly hides. Early action drives the business.
